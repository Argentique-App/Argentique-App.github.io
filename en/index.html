not a marketing claim

no data leaves,
and you can check

Most apps promise not to send anything. This one cannot: the published build is compiled without android.permission.INTERNET. Without it the system denies every socket at the kernel level, whatever part of the code might attempt one — including a third-party library I didn't write.

$ aapt2 dump permissions argentique-release.apk package: com.argentique.app uses-permission: com.android.vending.BILLING # android.permission.INTERNET: absent # android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: absent

Don't take my word for it: download the APK and run the command. The development build does keep the permission — which is what makes the check interesting, since both come from the same source.

So there is no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising. And your archive is never copied: Argentique reads the original .zip directly, and never modifies it.

the waybackmachine

pick a month.
the whole app folds into it.

Posts, stories, conversations, right down to the contents of each thread: everything filters to the chosen month, and the interface takes on the colours that year had, in light or dark.

The picker only offers years actually present in your archive, and marks with a dot the months you posted in. Most archives only cover a handful of months — better to see that straight away.

the retrospective

your retrospective,
computed on your phone

Year-in-review features are computed on a server that reads your data. This one is computed offline, from a file you downloaded yourself.

Year by year or across the whole archive: the person you wrote to most, the accounts you followed from one end to the other, the date of your very first message, the hour you actually wrote at, the rich conversations that went quiet.

And when you share a card, there's a switch. "The person you wrote to most" isn't only your data — it's theirs too. Switched on, only initials leave the phone.

before you install

what Argentique cannot do

  • The export only contains what you did. Likes received, views on your stories, comments people left you are not in it. No app can show you those, and this one doesn't pretend.
  • It is not a backup tool. Argentique reads your files, it doesn't replace them. Keep your .zip archives.
  • Archive depth isn't up to it. Some exports go back to the creation of the account, others start much later. The app tells you what it received; it can't invent what's missing.
  • Android only for this first release.
